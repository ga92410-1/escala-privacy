Privacy Policy

Escala collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party services.

What stays only on your device: the names you type, round history, records, “house words” and your settings. They are never sent to anyone. Deleting the app erases them.

Multi-phone play: the app uses the local network (Wi-Fi or hotspot) so that nearby phones can find each other and share the game state (names, words played). This traffic stays on the local network, between the players; no external server is used.

Contact: if you write through the Contact button, your email is used only to reply to you.

Política de privacidad
